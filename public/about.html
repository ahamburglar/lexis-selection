About

Kidswear sale notes, gathered in one place.

Lexi's Selection is a curated kidswear sale finder for parents who like boutique brands but do not want to open dozens of store tabs every time a sale starts.

What the site does

The site collects public sale listings from third-party boutiques, normalizes prices and discounts, and lets visitors filter by brand, store, gender, category, size, and discount level.

What gets highlighted

I focus on harder-to-track kidswear brands, deeper markdowns, useful remaining sizes, recent price drops, and stores that are worth checking before sizes sell through.

What it is not

Lexi's Selection is not a store. Purchases, payment, shipping, returns, taxes, and final item availability are handled by each linked retailer.

Why the notes exist

Boutique kidswear sales can be scattered across small shops, seasonal outlet pages, final sale sections, and short weekend promotions. This site is meant to make that browsing process easier by grouping current finds into a searchable view.

Product details can change quickly. Always confirm the final price, size, shipping threshold, tax, return policy, and coupon status on the store's own checkout page before buying.
