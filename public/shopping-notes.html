Shopping notes

How to read boutique kidswear sale listings.

A high discount is only one part of a good buy. Sizes, shipping, return rules, and coupon stacking can change the real value of a sale find.

Discount depth

For many boutique brands, 40% off is common at early sale, 50%-60% off is worth checking, and 70%+ off is usually where sizes move fastest.

Size availability

A strong markdown is more useful when the remaining sizes match a real need. Filters help narrow finds by baby, toddler, kids, and larger kids sizing.

Price drops

Price-drop flags help separate older sale inventory from items that appear to have become cheaper since the last saved scan.

Store reliability

Trusted and caution notes are based on available shopping experience notes. They are informational and should be checked with current store policies.

Sale calendar idea

Many boutique brands follow seasonal markdown patterns: first sale, extra code, final sale, and outlet cleanout. Lexi's Selection will keep adding data-style notes so shoppers can compare when and where specific brands tend to drop lower.
