How it works

Sale finds are organized so they are easier to compare.

Lexi's Selection turns public boutique sale pages into a searchable list of current kidswear finds. The goal is quick comparison, not replacing the store's own product page.

01

Stores are checked

Public sale pages from boutique retailers are scanned for product titles, sale prices, original prices, sizes, images, and links.

02

Finds are filtered

Listings are grouped by brand, source, size, category, gender, discount, and whether a price appears to have dropped recently.

03

Store notes are shown

When available, store promos, trusted-store notes, shipping cautions, and sale wording are displayed beside the product card.

04

Checkout stays with the store

Every "View at store" button sends visitors to the retailer. The retailer controls checkout, payment, shipping, returns, taxes, and final availability.

What to verify before buying

Before purchasing, check the linked store's final checkout total, sale code, shipping threshold, tax, return terms, final sale status, and expected shipping timeline.
