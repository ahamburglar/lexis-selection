Editorial standards

How sale finds are selected, labeled, and maintained.

Lexi's Selection is built around practical curation: show the sale listings that are most useful to compare, add context where automated data is incomplete, and keep the final buying decision with the original retailer.

Store coverage

Stores are included when they carry boutique kidswear brands that are commonly searched by parents, have public product pages, and show enough pricing information to compare markdowns.

Brand focus

The brand list favors labels where discounts are difficult to monitor across small shops: Misha & Puff, Konges Slojd, Rylee + Cru, Louise Misha, Bonpoint, Noralee, Bebe Organic, and similar boutique brands.

Human notes

Trusted-store and shipping-risk labels are not generated from a single product feed. They are maintained as editorial notes based on known ordering experience, repeated shopper reports, and corrections sent to the site.

01

Collect public sale data

Listings are read from public boutique pages and normalized into comparable fields: brand, product name, sale price, original price, discount, available sizes, image, source store, and store link.

02

Remove weak matches

Items with missing prices, abnormal price ratios, unrelated brands, or unclear product information are excluded from the main deal list when they cannot be compared reliably.

03

Highlight useful signals

Recent price drops, new finds, deeper discount bands, trusted stores, shipping cautions, and size filters are surfaced because they help a shopper decide what is worth opening first.

04

Keep corrections open

Broken links, incorrect sizes, store-name changes, missing Instagram links, and outdated trust notes can be sent through the contact page and are handled as maintenance items.

What appears in the main list

Signal How it is used What to verify at the store
Discount depth Used to shortlist items at 40%, 50%, 60%, 70%, and deeper markdowns. Final sale status, coupon stacking, tax, and checkout total.
Price drop Shows when the current sale price is lower than a previously saved price for the same listing. Whether the lower price still applies after choosing size and color.
New find Marks products that were not present in the previous saved scan. Whether the item is truly in stock, not sold out after clicking through.
Trusted store Indicates a store that Lexi's mom or close friends have ordered from successfully. Current shipping timeline and return policy, since operations can change.
Shipping risk Flags stores with repeated shopper reports of slow shipping or order issues. Recent reviews, shipping estimate, and whether the price is worth the wait.

Maintenance schedule

The site is updated through saved scans rather than live checkout scraping. A saved scan records the product set, prices, and source notes at the time it was generated. Between scans, store pages can change, so the listing should be treated as a discovery tool rather than a guarantee.

Manual maintenance includes adding or removing stores, correcting Instagram links, updating trusted/risk labels, improving size filters, and removing sources that no longer provide usable product data.
