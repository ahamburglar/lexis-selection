Size guide

How to use size filters without overtrusting them.

Boutique listings use many size formats: months, years, European numbers, youth sizes, one size, and adult letter sizing. Lexi's Selection groups them for browsing, but the store's product page is always the final source.

How sizes are grouped on the site

Filter group Common labels included Notes before buying
Baby NB, 0M, 3M, 6M, 9M, 12M, 18M, 24M Baby sizing varies widely by brand. Check weight/height charts when possible.
Toddler 2Y, 2T, 3Y, 3T, 4Y, 4T Some brands use 2Y and 2T differently. For final sale, verify measurements if the style is fitted.
Kids 5Y, 6Y, 7Y, 8Y, 9Y, 10Y, 12Y, 14Y Dresses and sweaters can often flex longer than fitted pants, swim, or rompers.
European numeric 80, 86, 92, 98, 104, 110, 116, 122, 128, 134, 140 European sizes usually map to height in centimeters, but brand fit still matters.
Women / adult XS, S, M, L, XL, 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 Used for women's pieces carried by kids boutiques. These are not mixed into kids filters unless selected.

Safer final-sale buys

Cardigans, oversized knits, relaxed dresses, bloomers, bags, hair accessories, and pajamas often have more size forgiveness.

Higher size risk

Shoes, swim, tights, fitted pants, one-piece rompers, and narrow European brands need more checking before final sale checkout.

When to size up

For seasonal pieces, check whether sizing up still works with the weather window. A winter knit bought too large may miss the season.

Why a listing can appear in more than one size search

A product may include several size labels, or the store may write sizes in a title, variant name, and product option. The site tries to preserve useful matches so shoppers do not miss a size, but the product page should be checked before buying.

If a size appears incorrect or a product is showing in the wrong category, send the product link through the contact page so the parser can be adjusted.
