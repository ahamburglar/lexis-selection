Best time to buy Konges Slojd on sale.

Konges Slojd discounts are easiest to compare when you separate early seasonal markdowns from outlet-style cleanouts. This guide is a working reference for deciding when a price is worth opening.

Sale stage Typical discount What to check Buy or wait
First seasonal markdown 25%-40% off Popular prints, rainwear, swim, pajamas, baby gear, and matching sets. Buy only if the size or print is hard to find.
Mid-sale 40%-50% off Core clothing sizes, shoes, accessories, and pieces that sold out elsewhere. Good range for practical sizes and current-season favorites.

Where to check first

The Little Being, Smallable, Maisonette, and smaller boutique sale sections are useful starting points when comparing Konges Slojd markdowns.

What moves fastest

Recognizable prints, larger kid sizes, rainwear, swim, pajamas, and matching sets tend to move before the deepest markdown.

What can wait

Accessories, odd sizes, single-color basics, and past-season leftovers are more likely to reach the final markdown stage.

How I use this guide

I treat 50% off as the point where Konges Slojd starts becoming worth comparing across stores. At 60%+ off, I check size availability and shipping rules. At 70%+ off, I assume the item may sell quickly or be final sale, so I verify the checkout page before sharing or buying.

The exact timing changes by store. A boutique may hold inventory longer, apply an extra code for a holiday weekend, or move older pieces into an outlet section. This page is meant to help compare patterns, not predict every sale.
