Shopping checklist

What to check before buying a boutique kidswear sale item.

Deep markdowns can be worth it, but small-boutique sale buying has more variables than a normal store order. This checklist is the process I use before opening the checkout page.

01

Confirm the actual checkout price

Some stores show a sale price on the product page but remove an extra code at checkout, exclude certain brands, or apply a final discount only after size selection.

02

Read the final sale wording

Many boutique clearance sections are final sale. If sizing is uncertain, a slightly smaller discount from a store with returns may be less risky.

03

Check shipping threshold

A $20 discount can disappear if shipping is high. Compare item price, shipping, tax, and any free-shipping minimum before deciding the deal is better.

04

Look at size and season

For kidswear, the best markdown is not always the best buy. Consider when the child will wear it, whether the brand runs narrow, and whether the item can be layered.

Deal quality checklist

Question Why it matters When to be cautious
Is the discount 70% or more? For many boutique brands, 70%+ is where the price becomes meaningfully different from standard seasonal sale. If the item has only one hard-to-use size or shipping removes most of the savings.
Is it a recent price drop? A fresh drop can mean better value than a listing that has sat at the same sale price for weeks. If the product page has sold out after the saved scan.
Is the store familiar? Known stores reduce uncertainty around shipping speed, packaging, and customer service. If there are repeated reports of delayed shipping or unanswered emails.
Is the size flexible? Room-to-grow items like sweaters, dresses, and cardigans are safer than fitted swim, shoes, or one-piece outfits. If the product is final sale and the brand runs small or narrow.
Is the item easy to resell or gift? A recognizable brand in a popular print or neutral color has less downside if sizing is off. If the style is seasonal, personalized, fragile, or unusually shaped.

Best sale categories

Dresses, sweaters, cardigans, bloomers, leggings, swim, pajamas, and outerwear can all be worth checking, but the sizing risk changes by category.

Most common mistakes

Forgetting shipping, missing final sale rules, assuming all brands use the same size scale, and buying only because the percentage looks high.

When to skip

Skip if the product page has unclear sizing, no visible return policy, a suspiciously large price error, or a store note that makes the order timeline uncomfortable.
